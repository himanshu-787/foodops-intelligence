# FoodOps Policies

## Refund Policy

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days. If an order is cancelled before the restaurant accepts it, the full amount is refunded. For cash on delivery orders, refunds are given as FoodOps credit.

## Late Delivery

If delivery is delayed by more than 30 minutes beyond the estimated time, the customer can claim a 10 percent discount credit on that order. Delays caused by heavy rain or strikes are not eligible.

## Order Cancellation

An order can be cancelled while its status is placed or confirmed. Once the status is preparing or out_for_delivery, cancellation is not possible. When an order is cancelled, the stock is returned to the restaurant menu.

## Delivery Fee

Delivery is free on orders above Rs 299. Below that, a flat fee of Rs 30 applies. A surge fee may be added during peak hours, 7 PM to 10 PM.

## Payment Methods

We accept UPI, credit and debit cards, and cash on delivery. Cash on delivery is limited to orders up to Rs 2000.

## Wrong or Missing Items

If an item is missing or wrong, report it within 24 hours from the My Orders page with a photo. Support will review it and offer a replacement, credit, or refund.

## Account and Password

Reset your password from the login page using your registered email. An account is locked for 15 minutes after 5 failed login attempts.

## Allergies and Dietary Info

Restaurants mark items as veg or non-veg. FoodOps cannot guarantee that any food is allergen-free. Mention allergies in the order notes and contact the restaurant directly.
